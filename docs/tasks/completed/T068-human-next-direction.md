# T068 completion — scientific next direction

Completed October 5, 2026 by the scientific lead.

## Decision

No current method or tested fixed combination is accepted as a sufficiently
accurate modern abbreviation-extraction system. Ab3P remains the operational
incumbent as a pragmatic choice, not as a claim of proven contemporary
superiority.

The tested Schwartz–Hearst/PLODv2 exact union is rejected for adoption because
its recall gains bring too many false positives. This result rejects that
unconditional combination; it does not rule out every future selective,
learned or structure-aware approach.

The next direction is intentionally limited to cross-method error analysis and
the design of new methods, including an LLM-based approach. No implementation,
large-scale processing or new evaluation campaign is authorized by this
decision alone.

The complete rationale and method-level qualifications are preserved in the
[T068 decision](../T068-human-next-direction.md). The frozen
[T067 readout](../../T067-fresh-evaluation-readout.md) remains unchanged.
